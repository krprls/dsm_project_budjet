Brightleaf Tea Co.
Q3 Business Review (July to September 2026)
Sample document for training. Brightleaf Tea Co. is a fictional company.

Summary
Brightleaf sold 11,177 units in Q3 for total revenue of Rs 41,70,273 across Bengaluru, Mumbai and
Delhi NCR. Growth came almost entirely from Masala Chai Blend. Darjeeling First Flush fell
sharply in September after a supplier delay left it out of stock for three weeks.

Performance by product
Assam Breakfast: 3,277 units against a target of 3,300 (99% of target), revenue Rs 11,43,673.
Darjeeling First Flush: 1,171 units against a target of 1,500 (78% of target), revenue Rs 7,01,429.
Masala Chai Blend: 4,641 units against a target of 4,000 (116% of target), revenue Rs 13,87,659.
Chamomile Calm: 2,088 units against a target of 2,000 (104% of target), revenue Rs 9,37,512.

What went well
   Masala Chai Blend grew every month and is now the largest product by units.
   Instagram gifting posts drove a noticeable rise in first-time orders.
   Bengaluru remains the strongest city for every product.

What went wrong
   Darjeeling First Flush stockout in September. Orders were cancelled and customers complained.
   Chamomile Calm returns in Mumbai are far above every other product and city. Customers
   report crushed and wet boxes.
   Customer support replies slowed to three or four days during August.

Open questions for Q4
   Should we add a second supplier for Darjeeling before the festive season?
   Should we change the courier or the packaging for Mumbai orders?
   Is a monthly subscription for Masala Chai Blend worth launching this year?
   How do we prepare support for the Kashmiri Kahwa launch in November?
