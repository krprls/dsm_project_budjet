BRIGHTLEAF TEA CO. | KASHMIRI KAHWA LAUNCH BRIEF
Sample document for training. Brightleaf Tea Co. is a fictional company.

Launch date: 15 November 2026
Owner: Aisha (Marketing)
Budget: Rs 2,50,000

GOAL
Sell 1,200 units of Kashmiri Kahwa in the first 30 days and bring in 400 first-time customers.

PRODUCT
Green tea with saffron, cardamom, cinnamon and almond slivers. 100 g tin. Price Rs 549.

WHO IT IS FOR
Existing Masala Chai Blend buyers who want something special for winter, and people buying festive gifts.

CHANNELS
- Instagram: gifting reels and creator posts
- WhatsApp: message to past customers with an early-access link
- Email: two sends, one week before and on launch day
- Website: home page banner and a gift bundle with Masala Chai Blend

OPEN DECISIONS
1. Do we open pre-orders on 1 November or wait until stock is in the warehouse?
2. Do we ship Mumbai orders in the new thicker carton, which costs Rs 14 more per box?
3. How much of the budget goes to creators versus paid ads?

RISKS
- Stock arrives late, as happened with Darjeeling First Flush in September.
- Customer support is already slow and launch week will add more questions.
